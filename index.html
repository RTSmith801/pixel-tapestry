BetterTrack

BetterTrack is a responsive web application designed to help users track their daily routines, fitness activities, and wellness goals through a structured journaling and progress system. Built with React, the app features secure login authentication, a motivational Quote of the Day (QOTD) fetched from an external API, and an interactive calendar interface that allows users to create, edit, and delete daily task cards. Each entry supports customizable fields—such as title, quantity, weight, and notes—making it ideal for tracking workouts, health habits, or personal milestones. The application leverages React’s useState and useEffect hooks for state management and dynamic UI updates, with styling implemented in SASS for a cohesive, modern aesthetic.

About Me

I'm a software developer and a Computer Science graduate with an unconventional path into tech. Before returning to school to earn my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Weber State University, I built a career across a wide range of industries from maintaining heavy equipment and welding, to technical support, bartending, and client relationship management in finance. Each role taught me something different, but they all reinforced the same passion: solving problems and building things that make people's lives easier.

My transition into software began through independent game development, where participating in game jams introduced me to C# programming, rapid prototyping, and the excitement of bringing ideas to life under tight deadlines. That experience ultimately inspired me to pursue computer science full-time, and since then I've continued expanding my skills through professional web development, personal projects, and academic work.

When not behind a computer screen, you'll usually find me snowboarding, hiking, rock climbing, or training in jiu-jitsu. Always chasing the next challenge.

Thanks for checking out my site!
- Rick
